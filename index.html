I'm Abdullah — a digital marketer and freelancer who works across the full funnel: strategy, creative, campaigns and the tech that holds it together.

My day-to-day spans Meta Ads campaign creation and optimisation, social media management, creative design and lead generation — including inbound call campaigns. Alongside marketing, I build and ship websites, handle SEO foundations, and design AI-powered workflows using modern AI tools and prompt engineering to move faster without losing quality.

I also build internal business tools — most recently an in-house OMS (Order Management System) — because good marketing only scales when the operations behind it do.
